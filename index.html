Edit and publish your own short videos, in one place.

Sofiane.ecomlab is a personal desktop tool used by its owner, the creator behind the @sofiane.ecomlab accounts. It turns his raw face-camera recordings into finished vertical videos and publishes them to his own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts through the official APIs.

See how it worksPrivacy policy

Features

Everything happens on the owner's computer. Nothing is posted without his explicit click.

Video editing

Cuts silences, adds word-by-word subtitles and a title banner, renders a 1080×1920 vertical video.

Quality check

Every edit is reviewed (sound level, pacing, framing) before it can be marked ready.

Multi-platform posting

One ready video, one caption, three platforms: TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

Scheduling

The owner picks a date and time; the post leaves at that time, only on the accounts he selected.

How it works

The complete flow, from recording to publication.

1

Record

The owner films himself and drops the file into the tool.

2

Edit

The tool edits the video; the owner watches it and approves it.

3

Connect

Once, the owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes his own account.

4

Publish

He writes the caption, selects the platforms and clicks “Publish” or schedules it.

TikTok integration

Sofiane.ecomlab uses TikTok for Developers products only on the owner's own account, after he signs in and grants consent.

Product / scopeHow it is used
Login Kit · user.info.basicThe owner signs in with TikTok; the tool shows his display name and avatar to confirm which account is connected.
Content Posting API · video.uploadSends a finished video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft, so he can finish it in the TikTok app.
Content Posting API · video.publishPosts a video he approved directly to his profile, with his caption and the privacy level he chooses.

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).

Your data

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, never on a server and never shared. Videos and statistics stay local. No data is sold or used for advertising. Details in the privacy policy and the terms of service.
